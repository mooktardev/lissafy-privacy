# Lissafy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Lissafy is a personal budgeting app. It is designed so that **your data stays on your phone**.

## Data processed

Lissafy only stores what you enter: transactions (amounts, categories, notes, dates), accounts, budgets, goals, debts and loans, recurring transactions, settings and, if you provide it, your first name.

This data is stored **locally, on your device**. Lissafy has no user account and no server: we do not receive, collect or sell any of your data.

## What never leaves the phone without your action

- **Backups**: they are written to the phone (and included in the device’s iCloud or Google backup if you have turned it on). If you choose a folder (for example Google Drive), a copy is placed there. With a password, they are encrypted (AES-256-GCM); the password is never stored.
- **Exports and sharing** (PDF report, CSV spreadsheet, backup, error report): they go through the phone’s share sheet, to the app you choose.
- **Error report**: if the app crashes, a technical description of the error (message, screen, app and system version) is kept on the phone. It is only sent if you tap “Send”, and it contains no financial data.

## Home screen widget and notifications

- **Home screen widget** (Android): it is used to add an expense and shows no financial data (neither amounts nor your budget), only the month and the days left.
- **Notifications** (reminders, budget alerts, quick-add shortcuts): they are scheduled on the phone, without a server. The quick-add shortcuts show neither amounts nor your budget.

## In-app purchases (Lissafy Plus)

Lissafy Plus and tips are purchased through **Google Play**. Payment is handled entirely by Google, under its own privacy policy; Lissafy receives neither your payment details nor your identity. The app only asks Google Play whether Plus has been purchased on your account, to turn it on (and to find it again on a new phone with “Restore my purchases”). No third-party service is used.

## Permissions requested

- **Biometrics (fingerprint, Face ID)**: only if you turn on biometric unlock. The check is done by the system; Lissafy receives no biometric data.
- **Notifications**: only if you turn on reminders or quick-add shortcuts. They are scheduled on the phone, without a server.
- **Camera and photos**: only when you attach a receipt to a transaction. The photo is copied into the app’s private storage, on the phone.
- **Files**: only when you import a statement or restore a backup, for the file you choose.

The app also reads the phone’s language setting to show its text in English or French. This setting never leaves the phone.

## Your rights

Your data belongs to you and is entirely under your control: you can export it at any time (Statements, Backup) and delete it (Settings → Erase everything, or by uninstalling the app).
